About

I graduated from the University of Oxford, Christ Church College in 2025 with an integrated Master of Mathematics and Computer Science (MMathCompSci). I am currently a Research Engineer at LIG Defence & Aerospace, serving as part of my mandatory military service for the Korean government until July 2028. At work, I (1) am currently developing AI solutions for autonomous maritime and multi-domain systems in collaboration with Palantir Technologies, and (2) maintain and refine our internal multi-OS framework, which provides core functionality across the company’s defence systems.

My research interests live on the border between mathematics and computer science. I opt for a mathematical approach to CS problems, looking for mathematically ‘elegant’ representations of CS. Currently my interests split across two fields: knowledge representation (using maths and computation to find connections between separate topics) and genetic algorithms (using simple mathematical rules to grow elegant models and solutions).

Outside of work and academics, I enjoy travelling and eating, with my most recent trips being in Marrakech, Morocco and Oita/Beppu, Japan. I also spend a lot of free time building projects with Claude Code, and am currently working on creating an artificial language evolved through genetic algorithms and simulation (check below).

  1. 01
    Iterated Self-Evaluation Impossibility for Large Language Models
    J. Suh et al.  ·  IEEE Access  ·  in preparation, 2026
    Deciding whether a model’s own reasoning loop converges to an answer it endorses is Σ₂-complete, strictly harder than the halting problem.
  2. 02
    Byzantine Monitors in AI Control: A Threshold Hierarchy for Budgeted Oversight
    J. Suh et al.  ·  JAIR  ·  in preparation, 2026
    Computing the threshold for number of corrupt agents colluding with an AI agent they should be overlooking, three closed-form thresholds decide whether a budgeted auditor can still catch sabotage.
  3. 03
    Truthful Task and Resource Allocation in Leader–Follower Agent Organisations
    J. Suh et al.  ·  JAAMAS  ·  in preparation, 2026
    A money-free mechanism that makes self-interested agents report their competence truthfully; validated on LLM agents, whose misreporting falls from 54% to 12%.
  4. 04
    What a recording site is worth: measuring the drivers of cross-site generalisation in passive sonar vessel classification with a pool of evolved features
    J. Suh et al.  ·  Applied Acoustics  ·  in preparation, 2026
    Research into utilising evolutionary algorithms for ship-sound classification. Traditional ship-sound classifiers report 97–99% accuracy on single recording sites; we try to extend our model to apply to multiple sites simultaneously.
  5. 05
    Register-Invariant Jamo Coding for Short Korean Text
    J. Suh et al.  ·  ICTC 2026  ·  in preparation, 2026
    Developing the M0 Korean register-invariant jamo encoder, for efficient encoding of short Korean text compared to UCS-2 by 37% with a 42B register.
  6. 06
    [Digital Twin-Verified Design and Development of a USV Swarm Command and Control System]
    W.-S. Kim et al. (incl. J. Suh)  ·  JKIICE  ·  in preparation, 2026
    Digital twin-based verification of an AI command and control system for unmanned surface vessel swarms.
  7. 07
    Designing the VG8: An FPGA-Based 8-bit Game Console for Comparative Analysis of 1980s Personal Computers and Game Consoles
    J. Suh  ·  MMathCompSci Thesis, University of Oxford, 2025
    A simplified 8-bit game console built from scratch in Verilog on an FPGA, used as a platform to compare the hardware trade-offs of 1980s consoles and personal computers.

Language Evolution Simulator

ongoing

A simulation in which agents start with no language and evolve one through selection pressure alone. The current language consists of a 5-vowel, 12-consonant sound system, rigid word-order grammar, an evidential system, graded number, and ~84% who-did-what accuracy.

Genetic Algorithms  ·  Computational Linguistics

Etymology Tree Visualisation

completed

Constructing a searchable etymology tree (including 2,000,000 words across 4,502 languages) built from Wiktionary to easily and efficiently visualise the etymological evolution of words.

suspended

Research into the undeciphered 15th-century Voynich Manuscript. Statistical fingerprinting that resists frequency-matching shows the text looks generated by a template, rather than a cipher for a natural language.

Pokémon Ecosystem Simulator

A Pokémon simulator in which Pokémon are left to roam inside a virtual world. The simulation reveals how food chains, population booms, and extinctions emerge from the simple rules behind Pokémon interactions.
